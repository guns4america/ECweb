About the Author

Hi, I’m Erica, I believe that the words I share come through the power and guidance of God. Journey to Surrender is a collection of poems and reflections that trace the path of letting go, trusting God, and finding peace in His presence. My writing comes from my own experiences of love, loss, and spiritual awakenings. Through this book, I hope to encourage others who may be walking through similar valleys, reminding them that surrender is not weakness, but the doorway to strength, freedom, and hope. Beyond writing, I’m a mother, a woman of faith, and a lover of Scripture. Whether I’m studying the Bible with my women’s group, working hard in daily life, or simply reflecting on the quiet moments, I carry the same desire: to live authentically and create words that connect, comfort, and inspire. And to live with the Kingdom in mind. Thank you for being here. My prayer is that Journey to Surrender meets you where you are and points you toward the peace and love that only God can give.
